# Business Transformation Proposal for Shahrketab

## Transforming Underutilized Gallery Space into a Study & Work Space

---

## Project Overview

This project presents a Business Analysis proposal for transforming the underutilized gallery space of **Shahrketab** into a modern **Study & Work Space**.

The proposal aims to improve space utilization, enhance customer experience, diversify revenue opportunities, and support the long-term sustainability of the bookstore through a structured business transformation initiative.

The project follows a Business Analysis approach based on industry best practices and covers the complete lifecycle from business discovery to final recommendation.

---

## Business Problem

Shahrketab's gallery space is currently underutilized and generates limited business value.

At the same time, changing customer expectations and the growing demand for flexible study and coworking environments create an opportunity to redesign the space into a service that better supports both customers and business objectives.

---

## Project Objectives

- Improve utilization of existing gallery space
- Diversify business revenue opportunities
- Enhance customer experience
- Support long-term business sustainability
- Provide a structured Business Analysis recommendation
