# KPI Framework

## Business Question

How should the success of the proposed Study & Work Space be measured after implementation?

---

# Overview

The successful implementation of a business solution should be evaluated using clearly defined Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

This framework establishes a structured approach for measuring business performance, customer outcomes, operational effectiveness, and financial sustainability following implementation.

The KPIs proposed in this section are qualitative in structure and do not include target values, as those should be determined after implementation using actual operational data.

---

# KPI Design Principles

The selected KPIs should:

- Align with business objectives
- Be measurable and reliable
- Support informed decision-making
- Be monitored consistently
- Encourage continuous improvement

---

# KPI Categories

## Customer KPIs

These indicators measure customer experience and satisfaction.

### Suggested KPIs

- Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
- Repeat Visit Rate
- Customer Feedback Score

---

## Operational KPIs

These indicators evaluate day-to-day operational performance.

### Suggested KPIs

- Workspace Occupancy Rate
- Internet Availability
- Average Issue Resolution Time

---

## Financial KPIs

These indicators evaluate financial sustainability.

### Suggested KPIs

- Monthly Service Revenue
- Operating Cost Trend
- Revenue Growth Rate

No target values are proposed at this stage because financial data has not yet been validated.

---

## Business KPIs

These indicators measure progress toward strategic business objectives.

### Suggested KPIs

- New Customer Acquisition
- Customer Retention Rate
- Space Utilization Rate

---

# KPI Summary

| Category | Example KPIs |
|----------|--------------|
| Customer | Customer Satisfaction Score, Repeat Visit Rate |
| Operational | Workspace Occupancy Rate, Internet Availability |
| Financial | Monthly Service Revenue, Revenue Growth Rate |
| Business | Customer Retention Rate, New Customer Acquisition |

---

# KPI Monitoring

Each KPI should include:

- Measurement frequency
- Data source
- Responsible role

| KPI | Measurement Frequency | Data Source | Responsible Role |
|------|-----------------------|-------------|------------------|
| Customer Satisfaction Score | Monthly | Customer Surveys | Store Manager |
| Workspace Occupancy Rate | Monthly | Booking Records | Operations Staff |
| Monthly Service Revenue | Monthly | Financial Records | Finance Representative |
| Customer Retention Rate | Quarterly | Membership or CRM Records | Management |

---

# KPI Governance

Effective KPI management should define:

- KPI owner
- Review frequency
- Escalation trigger when performance deviates from expectations
- Responsibility for improvement actions

A governance approach helps ensure that performance measurement supports continuous business improvement rather than periodic reporting alone.

---

# Relationship with Previous Phases

This KPI framework is informed by outputs from the following project phases:

- Business Objectives

---

# Key Insights

- KPIs should evaluate customer, operational, financial, and strategic performance.
- Performance should be reviewed regularly to support evidence-based decision-making.
- Target values should only be established after collecting sufficient operational data.
- KPI governance strengthens accountability and continuous improvement.

---

# Traceability

This framework supports the following project phases:

- Final Recommendation
- Portfolio Package

---

# Limitations

This framework defines what should be measured but does not establish performance targets.

Actual KPI targets, benchmarks, and reporting dashboards should be developed after implementation using operational data and organizational objectives.
