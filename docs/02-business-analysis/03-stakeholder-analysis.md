# Stakeholder Analysis

## Overview

Stakeholder analysis identifies the individuals and groups that may influence, or be influenced by, decisions regarding the future use of Shahrketab's gallery space. Understanding stakeholder expectations is essential for developing a solution that balances business objectives with the bookstore's cultural mission.

---

## Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Key Interests | Power | Interest |
|--------------|------|---------------|--------|----------|
| Shahrketab Management | Strategic decision-maker | Business sustainability, profitability, cultural identity | High | High |
| Branch Manager | Operational manager | Feasible implementation, daily operations | High | High |
| Gallery & Cultural Team | Cultural program management | Preserving cultural activities and exhibitions | Medium | High |
| Current Customers | Existing bookstore customers | Service quality, affordable products, cultural experience | Low | High |
| Potential Study Space Users | Students, freelancers, professionals | Quiet environment, affordability, accessibility | Low | High |
| Employees | Daily operations | Workload, training, operational changes | Medium | Medium |
| Suppliers & Service Providers | Equipment and service providers | Stable business relationship | Low | Medium |
| Local Cultural Community | Artists and cultural partners | Maintaining community engagement and cultural events | Low | Medium |

## Key Stakeholder Considerations

Several external economic factors may influence stakeholder expectations throughout this project.

For example, persistent inflation and reduced purchasing power may affect customer spending behavior. Customers may reduce discretionary purchases such as mugs, stationery, decorative products, and other cultural accessories while placing greater value on affordable services that support studying or working.

This observation is treated as a business hypothesis rather than a confirmed fact and will be evaluated during the Market & Customer Research phase of this project.
