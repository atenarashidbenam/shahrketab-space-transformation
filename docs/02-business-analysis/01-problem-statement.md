# Problem Statement

## Background

Shahrketab operates a gallery space that supports exhibitions and cultural activities alongside its core retail business.

As customer behavior, market conditions, and the use of physical retail spaces continue to evolve, organizations increasingly need to evaluate whether existing assets are being used in ways that maximize both customer value and business value.

At present, there is insufficient evidence to determine whether the current use of the gallery represents the most effective long-term utilization of this space.

---

## Current Situation

- The gallery is primarily used for cultural activities and exhibitions.
- The bookstore aims to preserve its cultural identity while maintaining business sustainability.
- No structured evaluation has been conducted to compare the current use of the gallery with alternative business opportunities.
- No internal operational or financial data is available for analysis.

---

## Desired Situation

Management should be able to make an informed decision regarding the future use of the gallery based on structured business analysis rather than assumptions.

The decision should consider customer needs, market demand, financial feasibility, implementation risks, and alignment with Shahrketab's cultural mission.

---

## Business Problem

The organization currently lacks sufficient decision-support information to determine whether the existing gallery space is delivering its highest potential business value.

Without a structured evaluation, management cannot confidently compare the current model with feasible alternatives.

---

## Impact

If no structured evaluation is performed:

- Business opportunities may remain unidentified.
- Physical space may not be utilized optimally.
- Strategic decisions may rely on assumptions instead of evidence.
- Future investments may involve unnecessary risk.

---

## Success Criteria

This project will be considered successful if it provides management with:

- A clear understanding of the business problem.
- Evidence-based alternative evaluation.
- Financial feasibility assessment.
- Risk assessment.
- Actionable business recommendations.
