# Business Need

## Overview

To support future strategic decisions regarding the gallery space, Shahrketab requires a structured business evaluation based on evidence rather than assumptions.

The organization needs sufficient information to understand whether the current use of the gallery continues to provide the greatest value or whether alternative service concepts should be considered.

---

## Business Needs

The business requires:

- A clear understanding of customer needs and expectations.
- Market evidence regarding demand for alternative services.
- Competitor benchmarking.
- Financial feasibility analysis.
- Risk assessment of potential alternatives.
- A structured decision-making framework.
- Business recommendations supported by evidence.

---

## Expected Business Value

Meeting these business needs will enable management to:

- Make informed strategic decisions.
- Reduce uncertainty before investing in changes.
- Improve utilization of existing physical space.
- Balance commercial opportunities with Shahrketab's cultural mission.
- Support long-term business sustainability.
