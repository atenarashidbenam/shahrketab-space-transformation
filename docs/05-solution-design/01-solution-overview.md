# Solution Overview

## Business Question

What is the proposed solution, and how does it address Shahrketab's business problem?

---

# Overview

Based on the previous business analysis, market research, and evaluation of solution alternatives, the proposed solution is to transform Shahrketab's underutilized gallery into a flexible Study & Work Space.

The proposed concept aims to improve space utilization while creating additional value for customers and supporting Shahrketab's long-term business objectives.

This section provides a high-level overview of the proposed solution before presenting the detailed design in the following sections.

---

# Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is to redesign the existing gallery as a multi-purpose Study & Work Space that supports:

- Individual studying
- Remote work
- Reading
- Cultural engagement
- Flexible use of space

The concept combines productivity with Shahrketab's existing cultural identity, creating an environment that encourages learning, creativity, and community engagement.

---

# Solution Objectives

The proposed solution aims to:

- Improve utilization of the gallery space
- Create additional revenue opportunities
- Enhance customer experience
- Attract new customer segments
- Strengthen Shahrketab's cultural and educational positioning
- Support long-term business sustainability

---

# Core Features

The proposed Study & Work Space is expected to include:

- Quiet study areas
- Individual workstations
- High-speed Wi-Fi
- Power outlets
- Comfortable seating
- Reading-friendly environment
- Access to books
- Flexible seating arrangements
- Optional refreshments (subject to operational decisions)

# Solution Scope

## In Scope

The proposed solution includes:

- Gallery redesign
- Study and work facilities
- Seating arrangements
- Internet connectivity improvements
- Customer experience enhancements

---

## Out of Scope

The following items are outside the scope of this proposal:

- Expansion of the bookstore retail area
- Major building renovation
- Opening additional branches
- Large-scale digital transformation
- Changes to Shahrketab's core retail operations

---

# Assumptions

The proposed solution assumes that:

- Sufficient customer demand exists.
- The current gallery can be adapted without major structural modifications.
- Required investment is financially feasible.
- Customers value a combination of productivity and cultural experiences.
