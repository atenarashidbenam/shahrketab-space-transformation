# Service Blueprint

## Business Question

How will the proposed Study & Work Space service be delivered from both the customer and operational perspectives?

---

# Overview

A Service Blueprint illustrates how a service is delivered by connecting customer interactions with frontstage activities, backstage operations, and supporting business processes.

Unlike a Customer Journey, which focuses only on the customer's experience, a Service Blueprint also documents the internal activities required to deliver the service consistently and efficiently.

---

# Service Blueprint

## Stage 1 – Arrival

### Customer Actions

- Enter Shahrketab
- Approach the reception area

### Frontstage Activities

- Welcome the customer
- Provide information about available services

### Backstage Activities

- Prepare the workspace before opening
- Ensure reception is operational

### Support Processes

- Staff scheduling
- Daily operational checklist

---

## Stage 2 – Registration & Payment

### Customer Actions

- Select a service
- Register (if required)
- Complete payment

### Frontstage Activities

- Process registration
- Accept payment
- Explain service guidelines

### Backstage Activities

- Record transactions
- Update occupancy information

### Support Processes

- Payment processing system
- Customer records

---

## Stage 3 – Space Usage

### Customer Actions

- Select a workspace
- Study
- Work
- Read

### Frontstage Activities

- Assist customers when needed
- Maintain a quiet environment

### Backstage Activities

- Monitor facilities
- Resolve technical issues
- Check workspace availability

### Support Processes

- Wi-Fi management
- Facility maintenance
- Cleaning services

---

## Stage 4 – Optional Services

### Customer Actions

- Request assistance
- Purchase refreshments (if available)

### Frontstage Activities

- Respond to customer requests
- Provide optional services

### Backstage Activities

- Manage inventory
- Coordinate service availability

### Support Processes

- Supplier management
- Inventory management

---

## Stage 5 – Departure

### Customer Actions

- Leave the workspace

### Frontstage Activities

- Thank the customer
- Invite feedback (if applicable)

### Backstage Activities

- Update occupancy records
- Prepare the workspace for the next customer

### Support Processes

- Cleaning
- Routine maintenance

---

## Stage 6 – Follow-up

### Customer Actions

- Receive future updates or promotional messages (optional)

### Frontstage Activities

- Communicate with returning customers

### Backstage Activities

- Update customer records
- Review customer feedback

### Support Processes

- CRM system
- Marketing communication

---

# Service Blueprint Summary

| Stage | Customer Actions | Frontstage | Backstage | Support Processes |

# Key Service Principles

The proposed service should emphasize:

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Customer comfort
- Operational efficiency
- Consistent service quality

---

# Critical Service Risks

Potential operational risks include:

- Internet connectivity issues
- Overcrowding during peak periods
- Noise affecting customer experience
- Staff shortages
- Equipment or facility failures

These risks should be considered during implementation planning and risk assessment.

---

# Expected Outcomes

The proposed Service Blueprint is expected to:

- Standardize service delivery
- Clarify staff responsibilities
- Improve operational efficiency
- Support a consistent customer experience
- Reduce service delivery inconsistencies

---

# Key Insights

Delivering an excellent customer experience depends not only on customer-facing interactions but also on efficient backstage operations and well-defined support processes.

A structured Service Blueprint helps ensure that every stage of the service is aligned with the project's business objectives.

---

# Traceability

This analysis supports the following project phases:

- Customer Journey
- Financial Feasibility
- Risk Assessment
- KPI Measurement
- Implementation Roadmap

---

# Limitations

This Service Blueprint represents a conceptual operational model.

Detailed operational procedures, staffing plans, technology implementation, and service standards should be developed during the implementation phase.
