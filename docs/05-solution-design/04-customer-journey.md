# Customer Journey

## Business Question

How does a customer experience the proposed Study & Work Space service from initial awareness to post-visit engagement, and where are the opportunities to improve that experience?

---

# Overview

A Customer Journey maps the customer's experience across every stage of interacting with the proposed Study & Work Space.

Unlike the Service Blueprint, which focuses on operational processes, the Customer Journey focuses entirely on the customer's perspective, highlighting their goals, actions, interactions, pain points, and opportunities for improvement.

The objective is to understand how the service can deliver a consistent and positive customer experience throughout the entire journey.

---

# Stage 1 – Awareness

## Customer Goal

Find a suitable place for studying or working.

### Customer Actions

- Search online
- Hear about the service through recommendations
- Visit Shahrketab's website or social media

### Touchpoints

- Website
- Social media
- Word of mouth
- Physical storefront

### Potential Pain Points

- Limited service information
- Unclear pricing
- Lack of awareness

### Improvement Opportunities

- Clear service descriptions
- Active social media presence
- Transparent pricing information

---

# Stage 2 – Arrival

## Customer Goal

Understand how the service works.

### Customer Actions

- Enter Shahrketab
- Observe the environment
- Ask staff for information

### Touchpoints

- Reception
- Staff
- Directional signage

### Potential Pain Points

- Confusing layout
- Waiting time
- Difficulty locating available seats

### Improvement Opportunities

- Friendly customer greeting
- Clear signage
- Simple orientation process

---

# Stage 3 – Registration & Payment

## Customer Goal

Access the service quickly and easily.

### Customer Actions

- Select the desired service
- Register (if required)
- Complete payment

### Touchpoints

- Reception
- Payment system

### Potential Pain Points

- Slow payment process
- Complicated registration

### Improvement Opportunities

- Fast payment process
- Simple registration
- Clear pricing information

---

# Stage 4 – Study & Work Experience

## Customer Goal

Remain productive in a comfortable environment.

### Customer Actions

- Study
- Work
- Read
- Use available facilities

### Touchpoints

- Workspace
- Furniture
- Wi-Fi
- Power outlets
- Staff support

### Potential Pain Points

- Noise
- Poor internet connection
- Limited charging facilities
- Uncomfortable seating

### Improvement Opportunities

- Reliable Wi-Fi
- Comfortable furniture
- Quiet environment
- Regular facility maintenance

---

# Stage 5 – Departure

## Customer Goal

Leave with a positive impression.

### Customer Actions

- Finish the session
- Leave the workspace
- Provide feedback (optional)

### Touchpoints

- Staff
- Exit
- Feedback channel

### Potential Pain Points

- No feedback opportunity
- Unfriendly departure experience

### Improvement Opportunities

- Friendly farewell
- Simple feedback collection
- Appreciation message

---

# Stage 6 – Post-Visit

## Customer Goal

Decide whether to return or recommend the service.

### Customer Actions

- Follow Shahrketab on social media
- Recommend the service to others
- Plan another visit

### Touchpoints

- Social media
- Email communication (if applicable)
- Promotional campaigns

### Potential Pain Points

- Limited customer engagement
- No follow-up communication
- Lack of incentives for returning customers

### Improvement Opportunities

- Customer engagement campaigns
- Loyalty initiatives (if implemented)
- Regular communication

---

# Customer Journey Summary

| Stage | Customer Goal | Primary Touchpoints | Improvement Opportunity |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Awareness | Discover the service | Website, Social Media | Clear communication |
| Arrival | Understand the service | Reception, Staff | Easy orientation |
| Registration & Payment | Access the service | Reception, Payment | Fast and simple process |
| Study & Work Experience | Be productive | Workspace, Wi-Fi | Comfortable environment |
| Departure | Leave satisfied | Staff, Exit | Feedback collection |
| Post-Visit | Return or recommend | Social Media, Email | Customer engagement |

---

# Success Indicators

| Stage | Success Indicator |
|--------|-------------------|
| Awareness | Increased customer inquiries |
| Arrival | Short waiting time |
| Registration & Payment | Fast transaction completion |
| Study & Work Experience | High customer satisfaction |
| Departure | Positive customer feedback |
| Post-Visit | Repeat visits and referrals |

---

# Key Insights

- The customer experience begins before entering the physical space.
- The Study & Work Experience is the most critical stage for delivering customer value.
- Positive post-visit engagement can increase customer loyalty and encourage repeat visits.
- Continuous improvement across all touchpoints contributes to long-term customer satisfaction.
