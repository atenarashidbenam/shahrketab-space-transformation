# Space Layout

## Business Question

How should the proposed Study & Work Space be organized to support customer needs and business objectives?

---

# Overview

The purpose of the proposed space layout is to organize Shahrketab's gallery into functional areas that support studying, working, reading, and customer engagement.

Rather than focusing on architectural design or interior decoration, this section defines the functional zones required to deliver the proposed service effectively.

The layout is intended to maximize space utilization while providing a comfortable and productive customer experience.

---

# Functional Areas

## 1. Quiet Study Zone

### Purpose

Provide a quiet environment for individual studying and focused work.

### Key Features

- Individual study desks
- Comfortable chairs
- Power outlets
- Quiet atmosphere
- Appropriate lighting

---

## 2. Flexible Work Zone

### Purpose

Support freelancers and remote workers with a flexible and productive workspace.

### Key Features

- Shared work tables
- Reliable Wi-Fi
- Power outlets
- Comfortable workstations
- Flexible seating arrangements

---

## 3. Reading Area

### Purpose

Preserve Shahrketab's cultural identity by providing a welcoming space for reading and informal learning.

### Key Features

- Comfortable seating
- Easy access to books
- Calm environment
- Reading-friendly atmosphere

---

## 4. Reception Area

### Purpose

Provide customer guidance and support operational activities.

### Key Features

- Customer information
- Payment and registration (if required)
- General assistance
- Service guidance

---

# Space Allocation Principles

The proposed layout should follow these principles:

- Prioritize quiet areas for focused activities.
- Ensure easy movement between functional zones.
- Maintain clear visibility and accessibility.
- Preserve Shahrketab's cultural atmosphere.
- Allow flexibility for future modifications.

---

# Design Considerations

The proposed layout should consider:

- Customer comfort
- Noise management
- Lighting quality
- Accessibility
- Safety
- Operational efficiency

---

# Alignment with Customer Personas

| Functional Area | Primary Customer Persona |
|-----------------|--------------------------|
| Quiet Study Zone | University Student |
| Flexible Work Zone | Freelancer / Remote Worker |
| Reading Area | Book Lover & Lifelong Learner |
| Reception Area | All Customer Personas |

---

# Expected Outcomes

The proposed layout is expected to:

- Improve customer experience.
- Increase utilization of the gallery space.
- Support multiple customer segments.
- Strengthen Shahrketab's cultural identity.
- Improve operational efficiency.

---

# Key Insights

The proposed space layout is designed around customer needs rather than physical aesthetics.

Each functional area supports a specific business objective while collectively contributing to a flexible, productive, and culturally aligned customer experience.

# Limitations

This document defines the functional organization of the proposed space.

It does not include architectural drawings, interior design specifications, furniture selection, or engineering details.

These aspects would require further design work during implementation.
