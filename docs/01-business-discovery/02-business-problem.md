# Business Problem

## Current Situation

Shahrketab operates a gallery space alongside its primary retail business. The gallery supports cultural activities and exhibitions that align with the organization's identity and mission.

However, like many physical retail businesses, bookstores face increasing pressure to maximize the value generated by their available space while responding to changing customer behaviors and economic conditions.

## Business Problem

The business does not currently know whether the existing use of the gallery represents the most effective use of this physical asset.

Without a structured business evaluation, management cannot determine whether maintaining the current model or introducing an alternative service would create greater value for customers and the organization.

## Decision Problem

Should the existing gallery continue operating in its current form, or should alternative uses of the space be evaluated to improve business value while preserving Shahrketab's cultural identity?

## Project Assumption

This project does not assume that the current gallery is ineffective or financially unsuccessful.

Instead, it evaluates potential alternatives through business analysis, market research, financial modeling, and risk assessment.
