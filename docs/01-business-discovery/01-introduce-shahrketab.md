# Introduce Shahrketab

## Overview

Shahrketab is one of Iran's well-known cultural retail brands, combining the sale of books, stationery, and cultural products with educational and artistic activities. Beyond its retail function, Shahrketab aims to provide an environment that promotes reading, learning, and community engagement.

The selected branch for this case study includes a gallery space on its second floor. This space has traditionally been used for exhibitions and cultural events, providing additional value beyond the bookstore's core retail operations.

## Project Context

This project explores whether the existing gallery space could be repurposed into a hybrid study and coworking lounge while maintaining the cultural identity of Shahrketab.

The objective is not to assume that the current use of the gallery is ineffective, but to evaluate whether an alternative use of the space could create additional business value for both customers and the bookstore.

## Project Scope

This project is developed as a Business Analysis and Business Feasibility Study.

Because no internal operational or financial data from Shahrketab is available, all analyses will be based on:

- Market research
- Competitor analysis
- Customer research
- Clearly documented assumptions
- Business analysis techniques
- Financial scenario modeling

The purpose of this project is to support business decision-making rather than to evaluate historical company performance.
