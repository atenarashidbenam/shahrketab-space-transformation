# Customer Personas

## Business Question

Who are the primary customer segments that could benefit from Shahrketab's proposed Study & Work Space, and what are their needs, motivations, and challenges?

---

# Overview

Customer personas represent fictional but evidence-informed customer profiles that help understand different user groups.

These personas are developed using market research, industry trends, and reasonable business assumptions. They are intended to support solution design and should be refined if customer interviews or survey data become available.

---

# Persona 1 – University Student

## Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 18–27 |
| Occupation | University Student |
| Visit Frequency | Several times per week |
| Budget | Limited |

### Goals

- Study in a quiet environment
- Prepare for exams
- Access reliable Wi-Fi
- Stay for extended study sessions

### Needs

- Affordable pricing
- Comfortable seating
- Power outlets
- Reliable internet
- Quiet atmosphere

### Pain Points

- Crowded cafés
- Noisy public spaces
- Limited charging facilities
- High coworking membership fees

### Representative Quote

> "I need a quiet place where I can study for several hours without spending too much money."

---

# Persona 2 – Freelancer / Remote Worker

## Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 23–40 |
| Occupation | Freelancer / Remote Worker |
| Visit Frequency | Several times per month |
| Budget | Moderate |

### Goals

- Work productively
- Maintain focus
- Access stable internet
- Use a flexible workspace

### Needs

- Reliable Wi-Fi
- Comfortable workstation
- Quiet environment
- Coffee or refreshments (if available)

### Pain Points

- Distractions at home
- Isolation
- Expensive coworking memberships
- Lack of flexible workspaces

### Representative Quote

> "I want an affordable place with reliable internet where I can work without distractions."

---

# Persona 3 – Book Lover & Lifelong Learner

## Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Age | 25–50 |
| Occupation | Various |
| Visit Frequency | Weekly or monthly |
| Budget | Moderate |

### Goals

- Read books
- Spend time in a cultural environment
- Participate in cultural activities
- Occasionally study or work

### Needs

- Comfortable seating
- Calm atmosphere
- Access to books
- Inspiring environment

### Pain Points

- Limited public cultural spaces
- Difficulty finding places that combine reading and productivity
- Few environments designed for both learning and leisure

### Representative Quote

> "I enjoy spending time in places where reading, culture, and learning come together."

# Persona Comparison

| Persona | Primary Goal | Price Sensitivity | Expected Visit Frequency |
|----------|--------------|------------------|--------------------------|
| University Student | Studying | High | High |
| Freelancer / Remote Worker | Working | Medium | Medium |
| Book Lover & Lifelong Learner | Reading & Leisure | Medium | Medium |

---

# Economic Considerations

Current economic conditions in Iran may influence customer behavior across all three personas.

- University students are expected to be the most price-sensitive segment.
- Freelancers are likely to evaluate the balance between cost and productivity.
- Book lovers may prioritize experience and cultural value while remaining sensitive to pricing.

These assumptions should be validated through customer research before final pricing or service design decisions are made.

---

# Key Insights

- Three primary customer segments have been identified.
- Although their motivations differ, all personas value a quiet, comfortable, and productive environment.
- Affordability is expected to be a key success factor.
- The proposed concept should balance productivity, culture, and accessibility.

---

# Limitations

These personas are based on market research and business assumptions rather than direct interviews with Shahrketab customers.

Future customer surveys and interviews may refine these profiles and reveal additional customer segments.
