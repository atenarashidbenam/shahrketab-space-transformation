# Risk Assessment

## Business Question

What are the primary risks associated with implementing the proposed Study & Work Space, and how can they be managed?

---

# Overview

Every business transformation initiative involves uncertainty. Identifying potential risks before implementation helps improve decision-making, increase project preparedness, and reduce the likelihood of unexpected challenges.

This assessment provides a qualitative evaluation of the major risks that may affect the successful implementation of the proposed Study & Work Space and outlines potential mitigation strategies.

---

# Risk Categories

## Financial Risks

### Examples

- Higher-than-expected implementation costs
- Lower customer demand
- Rising operating expenses
- Inflation affecting profitability

### Potential Impact

- Reduced financial sustainability
- Delayed return on investment
- Budget overruns

### Possible Mitigation

- Begin with a pilot implementation
- Monitor costs regularly
- Review pricing strategy periodically

---

## Operational Risks

### Examples

- Staff shortages
- Inefficient space management
- Internet service interruptions
- Equipment failures

### Potential Impact

- Reduced service quality
- Poor customer experience
- Lower operational efficiency

### Possible Mitigation

- Staff training
- Preventive maintenance
- Backup internet connectivity
- Standard operating procedures

---

## Customer Risks

### Examples

- Lower-than-expected customer adoption
- Low repeat visit rate
- Customer dissatisfaction

### Potential Impact

- Reduced revenue
- Weak customer loyalty
- Negative reputation

### Possible Mitigation

- Collect customer feedback
- Continuously improve services
- Conduct targeted marketing campaigns

---

## Market Risks

### Examples

- Increased competition
- Changes in customer preferences
- Economic uncertainty

### Potential Impact

- Reduced competitiveness
- Lower market demand

### Possible Mitigation

- Continuous market monitoring
- Periodic service improvements
- Review competitive positioning

---

## Strategic Risks

### Examples

- Misalignment with business objectives
- Limited management support
- Failure to deliver expected business value

### Potential Impact

- Project underperformance
- Failure to achieve strategic objectives

### Possible Mitigation

- Executive sponsorship
- Regular project reviews
- KPI monitoring

# Key Insights

- Financial and market-related risks represent the most significant threats to project success.
- Most operational risks can be reduced through effective planning and preventive management.
- Continuous monitoring enables early identification of emerging risks.
- Regular KPI reviews support proactive risk management throughout implementation.

---

# Traceability

This analysis supports the following project phases:

- Financial Feasibility
- KPI Measurement
- Implementation Roadmap
- Final Recommendation

---

# Limitations

This assessment is qualitative and is based on the current understanding of the proposed solution.

No quantitative risk analysis or probability modeling has been performed due to the absence of detailed operational and financial data.

Future project planning should incorporate a detailed risk register supported by actual implementation data.
