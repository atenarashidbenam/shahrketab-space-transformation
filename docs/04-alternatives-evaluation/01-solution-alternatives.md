# Solution Alternatives

## Business Question

What strategic alternatives are available to address Shahrketab's business problem, and which option appears most promising for further evaluation?

---

# Overview

Before selecting a preferred solution, Business Analysis requires the identification and comparison of multiple feasible alternatives.

The following options represent different strategic approaches that could improve the utilization of Shahrketab's gallery space while supporting the organization's long-term objectives.

The purpose of this analysis is not to make a final decision, but to establish a foundation for objective evaluation in the next stage.

---

# Alternative 1 – Maintain the Current State

## Description

Continue operating the gallery without introducing significant changes.

### Advantages

- No additional investment required
- No implementation risk
- No operational disruption

### Disadvantages

- Underutilization of the gallery space remains unresolved
- No new revenue opportunities
- Limited improvement in customer experience

---

# Alternative 2 – Expand Retail Space

## Description

Convert the gallery into additional retail space for books, stationery, or cultural products.

### Advantages

- Consistent with existing business operations
- Simple operational model
- Utilizes existing retail experience

### Disadvantages

- Greater dependence on product sales
- Limited differentiation from the current business model
- Revenue may be influenced by changes in customers' discretionary spending

### Business Hypothesis

Current economic conditions may reduce customers' discretionary spending, potentially affecting demand for non-essential retail products.

This assumption should be validated through customer research and financial analysis.

---

# Alternative 3 – Event & Exhibition Space

## Description

Use the gallery primarily for cultural events, workshops, exhibitions, and community activities.

### Advantages

- Reinforces Shahrketab's cultural identity
- Attracts visitors
- Encourages community engagement

### Disadvantages

- Revenue may fluctuate depending on event frequency
- Requires continuous event planning
- Limited utilization between scheduled events

---

# Alternative 4 – Study & Work Space (Proposed)

## Description

Transform the gallery into a flexible Study & Work Space that combines productive work, studying, reading, and cultural experiences.

### Advantages

- Better utilization of existing space
- Opportunity to diversify revenue streams
- Supports students, freelancers, and lifelong learners
- Strengthens Shahrketab's educational and cultural positioning

### Disadvantages

- Requires initial investment
- Requires operational management
- Success depends on customer demand and pricing strategy

---

# Alternative Comparison

| Evaluation Criteria | Current State | Retail Expansion | Event Space | Study & Work Space |
|---------------------|--------------|------------------|--------------|--------------------|
| Initial Investment | Low | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Revenue Potential | Low | Medium | Medium | High |
| Customer Experience | Low | Medium | Medium | High |
| Space Utilization | Low | Medium | Medium | High |
| Cultural Alignment | High | High | High | High |
| Implementation Complexity | Low | Medium | Medium | Medium |

---

# Initial Assessment

Each alternative addresses the business problem from a different perspective.

The proposed Study & Work Space appears to offer the strongest opportunity to improve space utilization, diversify revenue, and enhance customer experience.

However, this observation should not be considered a final recommendation. A structured evaluation based on defined decision criteria, financial feasibility, implementation risks, and customer demand is required before selecting the preferred solution.

---

# Evaluation Criteria for Next Stage

The alternatives will be evaluated using the following criteria:

- Strategic Alignment
- Customer Value
- Financial Feasibility
- Operational Impact
- Implementation Complexity
- Risk Level

---

# Key Insights

- Multiple feasible alternatives exist for utilizing the gallery space.
- Each alternative presents different trade-offs between investment, operational complexity, and potential value.
- The Study & Work Space demonstrates strong potential but requires further evaluation before a recommendation can be made.

---

# Limitations

This assessment is qualitative and based on business assumptions and publicly available information.

Financial projections, customer research, and implementation analysis have not yet been completed and may influence the final recommendation.
