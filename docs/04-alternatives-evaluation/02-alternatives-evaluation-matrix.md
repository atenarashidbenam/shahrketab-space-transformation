# Alternatives Evaluation Matrix

## Business Question

Which solution alternative provides the greatest overall business value for Shahrketab based on defined evaluation criteria?

---

# Overview

After identifying feasible solution alternatives, the next step is to evaluate them using consistent decision criteria.

This qualitative evaluation matrix provides a structured comparison of each alternative. It supports objective decision-making and helps identify the most promising option for further financial and implementation analysis.

The evaluation is intended to guide decision-making rather than produce a final recommendation.

---

# Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Strategic Alignment | Alignment with Shahrketab's long-term business objectives |
| Customer Value | Expected value delivered to customers |
| Financial Feasibility | Potential financial sustainability and revenue opportunity |
| Operational Feasibility | Ease of day-to-day operation and management |
| Implementation Complexity | Difficulty of implementing the solution |
| Risk Level | Overall business and implementation risk |

---

| Score | Interpretation |

# Evaluation Summary

## Current State

Maintaining the current state minimizes investment and operational disruption but does not address the underlying business problem or improve space utilization.

---

## Retail Expansion

Retail expansion aligns with Shahrketab's existing business model but offers limited differentiation and may be affected by changes in customer spending behavior.

---

## Event & Exhibition Space

An event-focused approach strengthens Shahrketab's cultural identity and community engagement but may generate inconsistent utilization and revenue.

---

## Study & Work Space

The proposed Study & Work Space demonstrates the strongest balance between customer value, space utilization, strategic alignment, and revenue diversification.

However, its implementation requires additional investment and operational planning.

---

# Recommendation

Based on this qualitative assessment, the Study & Work Space appears to be the strongest candidate for further evaluation.

This conclusion should not be interpreted as a final decision. Financial feasibility, implementation planning, customer validation, and risk assessment should be completed before making a final business recommendation.

---

# Evaluation Assumptions

This evaluation is based on the following assumptions:

- Scores represent qualitative business judgments.
- No detailed financial analysis has been completed.
- Customer interviews and survey data are not yet available.
- Market conditions may change over time.
- The evaluation matrix may be updated if new information becomes available.

---

# Key Insights

- Multiple viable alternatives exist.
- Every alternative offers unique strengths and trade-offs.
- Structured evaluation improves decision transparency.
- The Study & Work Space demonstrates the highest overall potential while requiring additional validation.

---

# Limitations

This evaluation is qualitative rather than quantitative.

The assigned scores reflect current business understanding and publicly available information. Future financial analysis and customer research may lead to different conclusions.
